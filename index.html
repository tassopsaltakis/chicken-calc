How the chicken works

Is one chicken exact?

No. Chickens vary dramatically by breed, sex, and age. CHKN-1 is a practical house standard: 45 cm tall, 45 cm long, 75 cm across, and 2.5 kg.

How can a chicken equal liquid?

It cannot “hold” liquid. The calculator estimates whole-body displacement. For a fun working unit, it uses roughly 1 liter per kilogram, so a 2.5 kg chicken displaces about 2.5 L.

Can I use my own chicken?

Yes. Select Custom chicken or press Tune chicken, then enter your bird’s measurements. Custom values stay on this device.

Important: An unofficial novelty unit. Do not use for engineering, medicine, shipping, or coop welfare decisions. 1 CHKN = 1 selected reference bird
